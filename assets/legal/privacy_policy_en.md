# DocSafe Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

DocSafe is a document vault that works entirely on your phone. This policy explains what DocSafe does with your information. In short: your documents and details stay on your phone, encrypted, and DocSafe never sends them anywhere.

## What DocSafe stores

- The documents you import or scan, and their names, categories, folders, tags, dates, notes and reminders.
- Optional: text read from a document (only if you choose "Save for search") and your saved signature (only if you tick "Save on this device").
- Your settings, such as language and auto-lock time.

All of this is stored only on your phone. Each document is encrypted with its own key (AES-256-GCM); the keys are protected by the Android Keystore, and document details are kept in an encrypted database. Your PIN is never stored, only a protected verifier.

## What DocSafe does not do

- No account and no sign-in.
- No DocSafe servers. DocSafe does not have the Android internet permission, so it cannot upload anything.
- No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no selling or sharing of data.
- DocSafe does not read your contacts, location, photos or other files. It only opens the files you pick.

## Services provided by Google

- **Document scanner:** the camera screen is provided by Google Play services on your phone. The pages are processed on the phone and handed to DocSafe, which encrypts them. Google Play services is covered by Google's privacy policy.
- **Text recognition (OCR):** runs inside DocSafe on your phone with a model included in the app. No image or text leaves the phone.
- **DocSafe Premium:** subscriptions are bought and managed through Google Play. Google processes the payment under Google's privacy policy. DocSafe only learns whether a valid subscription exists; it never sees your payment details.

## Backups and exports

- **Encrypted backup:** you choose where to save the backup file (for example Google Drive, a USB drive or a folder). The file is encrypted with a backup password that only you know. If you save it to a cloud service, that service stores the file under your own account; DocSafe has no access to it.
- **Export:** an exported document is a normal, unencrypted copy saved where you choose. It is outside DocSafe's protection.

## Permissions

- Biometric: to unlock with your fingerprint or face, if you turn it on.
- Notifications: for reminders. Reminder notifications never show document names.
- Run at startup: to restore your reminders after the phone restarts.
- Google Play billing: to buy DocSafe Premium.
- Network state: added by Google's libraries; DocSafe itself has no internet access.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. Deleted documents go to "Recently deleted" for 30 days and are then removed permanently. "Erase all data" in Settings, or uninstalling DocSafe, permanently deletes everything and the keys. Because the keys never leave the phone, nobody, including the developer, can recover your documents without your phone and PIN, or without your backup file and its password.

## Children

DocSafe is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on DocSafe's Google Play page with a new effective date.
