DocSafe keeps your important documents — ID cards, licences, insurance policies, bills, certificates and marksheets — encrypted on your own phone, ready when you need them.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything stays on your phone. No account, no sign-in, no DocSafe servers.
• DocSafe has no internet permission, so it cannot upload your files.
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

STRONG PROTECTION
• Every document is encrypted with its own key (AES-256-GCM), protected by the Android Keystore.
• Names, tags and notes are kept in an encrypted database.
• PIN lock with optional fingerprint or face unlock, and auto-lock.
• Screenshots and the recent-apps preview are blocked inside the app.

ORGANISED AND QUICK TO FIND
• Categories, folders, tags, favourites and fast search.
• Premium: advanced filters (type, expiry, date added, category, tag) and batch actions — select many documents to favourite, move, categorise, export or delete at once.
• Issue and expiry dates with reminders before a document expires (Premium: automatically 7 days before every expiry date). Notifications don't show document names unless you turn that on.
• Scan paper documents into clean PDFs with the camera.
• Read text from a document on the phone (OCR, Hindi and English) to suggest the category and expiry date.

PDF TOOLS, ON THE PHONE
• Free: Photos to PDF — pick photos from your gallery or take them with the camera and get one PDF; edit pages (rotate, reorder, remove).
• Premium: Edit text — change the words in English PDFs in the document's own font; merge, split and compress PDFs; Annotate & Sign (add text, your signature, highlights or cover parts of a page). Results are always saved as a new copy.

YOUR BACKUP, YOUR CHOICE
• Create an encrypted backup file protected by a password only you know, and save it wherever you like — Google Drive, a USB drive or a folder. Restore it on the same or a new phone.
• A strength meter helps you pick a password that is hard to guess, and an optional reminder on the home screen tells you when a new backup is due.

HINDI AND ENGLISH
• DocSafe follows your phone's language and can be switched anytime.

DOCSAFE PREMIUM
Monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase, through Google Play: Edit text, merge, split and compress PDFs, Annotate & Sign, search inside documents, advanced filters, batch actions and unlimited reminders. Prices are shown in the app before you buy. Cancel anytime in Google Play.

PLEASE NOTE
• DocSafe is a private storage app. It is not DigiLocker, is not affiliated with any government, and does not verify documents.
• Your keys never leave the phone. If you lose the phone, reset it or forget your PIN, documents stored only in DocSafe cannot be recovered — keep an encrypted backup.
