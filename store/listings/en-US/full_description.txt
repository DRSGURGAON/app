Document Scanner Pro is a document scanner and PDF maker that keeps your scans in an encrypted vault on your own phone. Scan documents to PDF, turn photos into PDF, read text with OCR, sign PDFs and get reminders before ID cards, insurance or certificates expire. No account, no cloud, works offline.

📄 DOCUMENT SCANNER & PDF MAKER
• Scan paper documents into clean PDFs with automatic cropping.
• Photos to PDF: pick images from the gallery or take them with the camera and get one PDF.
• Edit pages: rotate, reorder and remove pages before saving.
• Import existing PDFs and images.

🛠️ PDF TOOLS ON YOUR PHONE
• Merge PDF, split PDF and compress PDF (Premium).
• Compress PDF or photo to a size you choose, like 100 KB or 50 KB, for form uploads (Premium).
• Sign PDF and annotate: add text, your signature, highlights or cover parts of a page (Premium).
• Edit text in English PDFs in the document's own font (Premium).
• Results are always saved as a new copy; your original stays untouched.

🔍 OCR TEXT RECOGNITION – HINDI & ENGLISH
• Read text from scanned documents on the phone, offline.
• Suggests the category and expiration date for you.
• Premium: keep the recognized text and search inside documents.

🔒 PRIVATE, ENCRYPTED DOCUMENT VAULT
• Every document is encrypted with its own key (AES-256-GCM), protected by the Android Keystore.
• PIN lock with optional fingerprint or face unlock, and auto-lock.
• No internet permission, so the app cannot upload your files. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• Screenshots and the recent-apps preview are blocked inside the app.

🗂️ ORGANIZE & FIND FAST
• Categories with icons, folders, tags, favorites and quick search.
• Premium: advanced filters (type, expiry, date added, category, tag) and batch actions.

⏰ EXPIRATION REMINDERS
• Reminders before your license, insurance policy, passport or certificate expires.
• Premium: automatic reminder 7 days before every expiration date. Notifications hide document names unless you turn them on.

☁️ ENCRYPTED BACKUP & RESTORE
• Save a password-protected backup to Google Drive, a USB drive or any folder and restore it on a new phone.
• Premium: automatic backup to a file you choose once (for example in Google Drive). Uploading is done by the app that owns that location.

🌐 HINDI AND ENGLISH
• Follows your phone's language; switch anytime.

⭐ PREMIUM
Monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase, through Google Play. Prices are shown in the app before you buy. Cancel a subscription anytime in Google Play.

PLEASE NOTE
• Document Scanner Pro is a private storage app. It is not DigiLocker, is not affiliated with any government, and does not verify documents.
• Your keys never leave the phone. If you lose the phone, reset it or forget your PIN, documents stored only in Document Scanner Pro cannot be recovered, so keep an encrypted backup.
